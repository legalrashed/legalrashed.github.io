Professional Biography

Bridging Complex Land Governance & Corporate Integrity

A.B.M. Rashedul Islam is a seasoned Legal Specialist and practicing Advocate based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. With extensive experience across both litigation and corporate legal advisory, he offers tailored solutions for complex property acquisition, corporate restructuring, and banking documentation.

His domain expertise centers deeply on Bangladesh Land Administration—a critical area requiring exhaustive scrutiny of land records (CS, SA, RS, BS, and City Surveys), Khatian verification, mutation history, and chain-title authenticity. He regularly acts on behalf of major financial institutions, real estate developers, and corporate entities to perform legal due diligence and mortgage vetting.

In conveyancing practice, Advocate Rashedul Islam drafts and executes high-stakes Saf-Kabla (Outright Sale) deeds, Irrevocable General Power of Attorney, Joint Venture Real Estate Agreements, Lease Deeds, and Trust Instruments. His thorough approach minimizes title defects and safeguards clients against future litigation risks.
